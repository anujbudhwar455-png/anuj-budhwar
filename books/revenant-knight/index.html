The Revenant Knight

He had a talent. A talent for ruining everything. Zael Vornath — the disgrace of a noble family — wasted his life in arrogance, bringing ruin upon those who loved him. Cast out, he discovered a terrifying truth… His talent was real. Forged in blood and battle, he rose as a mercenary unmatched by any blade. But fate had something far crueler in store. Captured by an Archlich and turned into a Death Knight, Zael became a weapon of destruction — slaughtering kingdoms… and his own family. Until the day he broke free. Until the day he killed the monster who made him. And then… He died. But death was not the end. He awakens in the past — before everything fell apart. This time, he remembers everything. This time, he will not fail.
