The Science of Clear Thinking

Why do intelligent people make terrible decisions? Every day we make choices about money, relationships, careers, and health. We believe we are thinking logically. But in reality, hidden psychological biases constantly shape our judgments without us noticing. In The Science of Clear Thinking, Anuj Budhwar explores 101 cognitive biases, emotional traps, and mental models that influence how we think and act. Through engaging stories, real-world examples, and powerful insights from psychology and philosophy, this book reveals the invisible mental shortcuts that often lead us astray. This is not just a book about psychology. It is a toolkit for clearer thinking in a complex world.
