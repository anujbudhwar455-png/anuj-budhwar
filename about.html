The person behind the pages

Pharmacy student by day, dark fantasy architect by night — building stories, songs, and software from Jaipur.

Also: studio, night sessions, and desk life in the gallery on the home page.

Hi — I’m Anuj Budhwar, based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. I graduated with a B.Pharm from Baba Mastnath University (Rohtak), and I’m now pursuing a Pharm.D (Post Baccalaureate) at NIMS University as a Pharm.D Intern.

Alongside clinical training, I write serialized dark cultivation and progression fantasy on Amazon KDP — worlds of forgotten heirs, revenge, and hard-won power. I write songs under the creative umbrella of Silent Heart Records, and I ship mobile tools under Desrein Studios.

The through-line is craft: whether a chapter, a chorus, or an app that respects the user’s device, I want the work to feel intentional and human.
